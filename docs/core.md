# Core Principles

1. **Fixed start-up resources** – The player begins with a set amount of coins sufficient to purchase a single random egg. No modal auto-appears on launch; the player starts the flow manually via Buy Egg.
2. **Random egg selection (spin-then-pay)** – Tapping Buy Egg opens a modal with a symbol-spinning animation; the player's tap freezes it and the resulting combination assigns the egg type (one of 16 possible dragons = 16 breeds). Tapping "Yo hoo" then deducts the egg price and adds the egg. Insufficient coins = Buy Egg disabled / "Not enough coins" state, no spin.
3. **Hatch timer** – After purchase the egg is ready to hatch after 1–2 days of real time; the timer is not displayed but is encoded at purchase. The egg gets its own detail screen (egg image + "Hatching...", Sell + Home only). Once due it shows "Ready to hatch!" with a green **Hatch** button — hatching is a manual tap, never automatic.
4. **Button navigation (no swipe)** – The UI consists of the main control screen plus one dragon detail screen per owned dragon or unhatched egg. Navigation is tap-only: the main hub has a bottom roster-strip of thumbnails, detail screens return via Home. There is no swipe/GESTURE logic (unreliable on the real device).
5. **Main control screen hub** – Central screen holding fixed icons that open modals:
   - **Buy Egg** (top-left) – purchases a new egg via spin-then-pay; disabled when coins are insufficient.
   - **Bewilder Beast** (top-right) – always visible; opens the boss intro modal with the boss image and a **Fight** button.
   - **Earn Coins** (bottom-left) – tap-to-collect hourly generation; the icon is shown **only when collectible coins are available** and opens the coin summary modal on tap. Nothing is credited without tapping.
6. **Dragon detail screen** – Shows a dragon's **Breed + age in the name ("Wooly Howl (2 days)"), Energy bar, and Strength stars** (5 str = 1 silver, 5 silver = 1 gold, max 125 base / 151 combat) centered below the bar, with a central image. No "Lv N · Age Nd" line. Egg screens show the egg + "Hatching..." (or "Ready to hatch!" + green **Hatch** button once due) and Sell + Home only. Hatched-dragon actions via modals:
   - **Train** – costs coins (scales with dragon strength) and drains a flat 15 energy; raises strength by a random amount. Blocked at 0 energy.
   - **Sell** – returns coins based on the dragon's age, strength, energy, and breed, plus a 50% refund on owned equipment.
   - **Monster** – opens the monster selection modal (0–3 spawned monsters).
   - **Equipment** – icon button (top-left) opens the per-dragon equipment page: 7 slots (head / wings / tail / one guard per leg), one piece per slot, bought with coins. Equipment adds to **combat strength** only (base strength, training costs, and the 125 cap are unchanged); stars everywhere show combat strength.
7. **Energy & recovery** – Energy **is a dragon's HP in fights**: every landed hit drains it, and a dragon at 0 HP is out. It recovers automatically over time; no manual action or food is required. A dragon with 0 energy cannot Train, fight monsters, or join Beast battles — except dragons dropped to 0 inside a Beast battle are **removed** instead of kept.
8. **Monster battles** –
   - Spawn-based: Easy spawns frequently, Medium rarely, Hard rarest; the selection modal shows only spawned monsters (0–3 rows) or an empty state.
   - Turn-based fight screen (see §14): the dragon attacks first, then the living monster hits back, until one HP pool reaches 0. Every attack rolls hit/miss first — hit chance is 80% on even combat, ±0.4% per point of combat edge, clamped to 55–95%. A landed hit deals `attackerCombat − floor(defenderCombat / 2) + random(0–8)`, floored at 3. Monster combat = base + its equipment bonus (Easy +0, Medium +8, Hard +23); monster HP pools are Easy 42, Medium 85, Hard 105.
   - **Win → receives coins (random, based on difficulty) plus a permanent strength gain** (Easy +1–2, Medium +1–2, Hard +2–4, clamped at 125). No egg reward. The dragon keeps its leftover HP as energy (a win still costs roughly a third of the bar — the monster lands hits back).
   - **Lose → knocked out: energy drops to 0** (must recover before fighting again)**; strength unchanged.**
9. **Training** – Paying the coin cost increases the dragon's strength by +2–5 (clamped at 125 max) and drains a flat 15 energy; cost scales with the dragon's current strength (config-defined). Requires energy > 0.
10. **Selling** – Provides coins whose amount depends on the dragon's age, strength, energy, and breed. Selling can supply coins for multiple egg purchases.
11. **Dragon turns against Bewilder Beast** – Round-based fight screen (see §14): every living dragon attacks in roster order, then the beast fires back at **one random living dragon** (uniform pick — it may focus-fire the same dragon twice). Same hit/miss + damage model as §8, with beast attack 85 and combat defense 46. A dragon at 0 HP is **removed** mid-fight. If all dragons are removed before the beast falls, the player loses with an empty roster and must buy a new egg (waiting for hourly coins if broke) — and the beast **heals back to full HP**, so a loss buys no progress and sacrificial rosters cannot grind it down.
12. **Bewilder Beast boss** – The final boss with an HP bar plus strength stars (display 101 = 4 gold stars: 75 base + 26 full-equipment bonus; combat defense 46 = 20 base + 26 gear). 580 HP. A fully equipped dragon hits it exactly as a bare dragon hit the old 20-defense beast; the documented roster is 3 fully equipped base-40 dragons (combat 66), which wins ~62% of fights (Monte Carlo, `engine/balance.test.ts`) — strong but no longer a guarantee; two dragons at 66 almost never win. Defeating it keeps survivors at their remaining (drained) energy, grants each dragon that attacked and survived a permanent +2–4 strength gain, and removes dragons dropped to 0; the beast vanishes for a day and then respawns. The killing blow counts as that dragon's beast win and earns it the reward coins on its battle record.
13. **Win condition** – Progress is measured by defeating monsters and the Bewilder Beast; there is no separate "win-end" state, the loop continues with new eggs and dragons.
14. **Fight screen + modal design** – Fights are a dedicated **full-screen fight screen, not a modal**: player dragons on the left, the enemy on the right, a green HP bar above every unit, fireballs flying attacker → target, hit bursts / MISS feedback, and a one-line turn narration. The engine resolves the whole fight instantly and persists it (anti-cheat — leaving mid-fight keeps the result); the screen only replays the returned turns at ~1.1s per turn. Tapping anywhere skips to the outcome; **Continue** appears only after the last turn. Other modals: no animation; semi-transparent dark overlay; every modal contains a close (X) button. Modals: egg-spin purchase outcome + "Not enough coins", training price/result, sell confirmation, monster selection, Bewilder Beast intro, and coin-earning summary ("Yo hoo" to close/collect).
15. **Hybrid coin generation** – Coins accrue hourly (unconditional, capped at 12h) PLUS 1 bonus coin per 100 watch steps (uncapped, koala-style), credited **only by tapping the Earn Coins icon** (visible only when collectible), enabling the first training after the first egg hatches.
